Secret DJ · ground-up redesign · turn 1 the committed direction · round screen

Hidden information should look like a held object, not a blank.

The governing idea: this game is made of things that exist and are being withheld — the DJ's identity, their claim, everyone's answers — and then handed over all at once with names attached. So the design's first job is a vocabulary for withheld that is visibly different from absent, and its second is a way of opening it. Not a mood, not a skin: secrecy as a rendering mode that every component can enter and leave. Everything else — density, the foreign embed, the arithmetic — is downstream of that. Below is the direction we're building, on the worst case: nine players, one on the floor, a long title, a timer that has already gone negative.

I'd ship 1a. Reasoning under each. 1c is the one I'd fight for if you want the game to feel dangerous instead of civilised.

▸ The your-track state inverts the whole palette: you are the only person in the room looking at a dark screen. It costs one token flip and it is the best three minutes of the game.

Wipe left-to-right, 420ms, one easing. The covers are the only thing that animates — no fades, no slides, no reflow. Reduced motion turns the wipe into a cut and the reveal is identical.

Why I'd ship it. It is the only one of the three where the secrecy language is also the layout: because a bar is the exact size of its secret, the round screen and the reveal are the same page with fewer covers, so the hardest screen and the payoff screen stop being two designs. It survives the worst case — no artwork, failed lookup and a nine-way tie all degrade to type on paper. It is measurably legible at arm's length across a room. And it earns its music reference by refusing to make one: the only thing on screen that looks like a media product is the embed, which now reads as evidence rather than as a styling failure. Risk: a room full of black bars can read as bureaucratic — a docket, not a party. The corrective is that the bars are temporary by definition and the paper underneath is warm, and that the copy stays dry rather than official.
